Research experience

  1. Applied ML Research, Accelx Inc.

    Jan 2025 — Present

    Machine Learning Engineer (safety-critical perception and language systems) · Dhaka, Bangladesh

    Appearance or body dynamics: recognising violent interaction in video

    • A Kinetics-pretrained R(2+1)D-18 on 2-second clips reaches 84.6% clip accuracy on the RWF-2000 validation split; a Transformer over YOLO11-Pose dynamics for up to four people (joint velocities, arm angles, an inter-person contact cue) reaches 61.7%, and a pose–RGB fusion model 69.0%.
    • Checking the pose features rather than the accuracy: about 70% of the "contact between two people" signals came from joints that were missing for both people (the pose model returns (0,0) for low-confidence joints), not from real proximity. Person slots are also not tracked between frames, so the model can report that a fight happened but not who did what.
    • Investigated motion-aware clip mining (dense optical flow plus pose heuristics) to reduce label noise from video-level annotations.
    • Caveat I keep with these numbers: checkpoints were selected on the same validation split they are reported on, so they are optimistic.

    Grounded and abstaining multimodal and LLM systems

    • Designed a pipeline in which the LLM writes prose only from facts computed deterministically, with every citation checked by a word-overlap test whose threshold was set from observed failures (about 17% overlap for misattributed citations vs. close to 100% for correct ones).
    • Built a staged image threat-analysis cascade (OCR first, a VLM only for hard cases) with knowledge-base verification, calibrated confidence, and handoff to human review.
    • Evaluated generated text with an LLM judge, ROUGE-L, and fact recall.

    Deployed systems

    • Real-time video safety analytics (weapon, violence, fall) for live multi-camera surveillance, served at sub-100 ms latency with TorchServe.
    • AI risk & content-safety platform: fine-tuned RoBERTa classifiers, deterministic intent aggregation, and grounded, cited Llama 3.2 alerts.
    • Multimodal content & threat analysis (PaddleOCR, Qwen3-VL) and zero-shot URL categorization (BART-MNLI, Redis).

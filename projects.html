Overcooked AAR

Telemetry vs. video

Pilot and follow-up study, September 2026. I had Video LLMs (Qwen2.5-VL and Qwen3-VL) write after-action reviews of real two-player Overcooked play twice, from the event log and from the gameplay video, and checked every timestamped claim against the log. On 15 episodes, claims from the log were 67–93% precise against 22–43% from video, and the video timestamps were no better than a random moment in the same minute. A perception probe (1,646 items over 76 episodes) shows why: the models read the clock and the score perfectly but cannot tell what a chef is holding (24–26% against 25% by chance for Qwen2.5-VL). The other 61 episodes and a blind human rating are next.

DocuVision

Document understanding

Scans, PDFs and phone photos in, structured JSON out. I fine-tuned LayoutLMv3-large on FUNSD and built a learned question→answer linker, which raised pair F1 on FUNSD (with ground-truth words) from 0.23 to 0.74. End to end on the raw, low-resolution scans with OCR it rose from about 0.15–0.17 to 0.43–0.46, because OCR noise dominates. The ablations taught me the most: with perfect tags the old rule-based linker still capped at 0.72, and grouping words in reading order was silently costing 25 F1 points on two-column forms. The model is on Hugging Face.

Speech Translator

Speech-to-speech translation

Speak English, be heard in Spanish, German or Japanese in your own cloned voice. I chose every component by evaluating it on 300 FLEURS sentences: a 100 MB CPU model beat NLLB-200 3.3B on German and Japanese, so the system routes per language. Named-entity masking, which sounded like obvious engineering, cost 3–6 BLEU, so I turned it off. A BLEU-6 collapse of NLLB-3.3B turned out to be a tokenizer that ignored the source-language token after a library upgrade; fixing it brought the score to 28.2.

Jarvis

Local voice agent

A voice assistant that runs entirely on my own hardware: wake word, Whisper, a tool-calling Qwen2.5-7B, memory and Piper, with 1.5 s median latency from the end of speech to first audio. I wrote a 20-case held-out test set after tuning the prompt, and tool-calling accuracy on it rose from 65% to 90%. The sandboxed tools blocked every unsafe command the model tried.

DepthCraft

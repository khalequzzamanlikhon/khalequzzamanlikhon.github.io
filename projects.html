Overcooked AAR

Telemetry vs. video

Pilot study, September 2026. I had one model write an after-action review of real two-player Overcooked play twice, from the event log and from the gameplay video, and checked every claim it made against the log. Over 15 episodes, log claims held up 81% of the time against 53% for video, and 98% of the video model's timestamps landed on a 5-second grid: it was spacing its claims evenly instead of reading the clock on screen. A blind human rating is planned next.
